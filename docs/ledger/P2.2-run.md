## Result

**The floor is met literally and the set is weak, and the record says both.** Five committed
scripted scenarios satisfy MC1, all five reach `DONE`, and all five hold their terminal state
across three repetitions. The floor of five replayable entries with at least two reaching `DONE`

## Why the weaker reading was not adopted as the verdict

Two reasons, and the first is the binding one.

**Imposing a distinctness rule now would amend a frozen contract after seeing the count.** MC1 was
frozen at `731d627` before any scenario was classified, precisely so the admission rule could not
be tuned to the answer. Discovering that the rule admits five near-identical entries is a finding
about the rule; acting on it inside this item would destroy the guarantee that freezing buys. The
same discipline forbids the opposite move: MC7 bars authoring new scenarios to diversify the set.

**And the criterion is the plan's, not this item's.** `docs/PLAN.md` §4 says "at least 5 replayable
entries, of which at least 2 reached `DONE`". It says nothing about distinctness. The gap between
"five entries" and "five independent observations" is recorded in `replay-set.md` under *The floor
counts entries, not information* and belongs to a plan revision to decide.

## What the met floor does not buy

The useful result of this item is not the floor. It is that **P3.2 is `inconclusive` anyway**.

P3.2 changes what the adversarial reviewer is asked: it sees only the diff and the other axes'
findings, and may only refute. MC5's second bar makes any such change inconclusive against a
corpus of frozen completions, regardless of how many entries that corpus holds — the cassettes
encode the old request and would miss. A met floor would have been misleading if it were reported
without that, which is why the two bars are stated as conditions in `replay-set.md` rather than as
caveats at the end.

P3.3 may be evaluable, conditionally: its change is to admission ordering and check comparison and
should not touch a model request. That has to be re-checked against P3.3's actual diff, and
`replay-set.md` says so rather than granting it in advance.
